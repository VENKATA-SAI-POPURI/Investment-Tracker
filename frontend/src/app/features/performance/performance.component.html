How these are worked out
  • Your return (XIRR) is money-weighted: it counts when you added and took out money, like the rate on a loan. Over less than a year it is annualised and swings a lot.
  • Time-weighted return (TWR) takes your deposits and withdrawals out, so it measures the investments alone. Growth of ₹100 charts it.
  • You vs an index mirrors every deposit and withdrawal into the index on the same day (the public market equivalent), so both sides have your timing.
  • vs Nifty 50 at the top always compares the whole portfolio since you started, with Home's rule: level or ahead is on track, up to 3 points behind is watch, more is act. Under 6 months it is not judged.
  • Assumptions check compares your time-weighted return since you started with the typical range the plan's long-run figures give over the same number of years. Under a year it is not judged. When you have run 3 points or more below the typical figure for 2 years or more, it offers your own return for the Plan.
  • Income is dividends, the interest part of P2P repayments, and FD interest and maturity gains, by the month they were paid. Top payers counts dividends only.
  • These are past returns, not a forecast.
